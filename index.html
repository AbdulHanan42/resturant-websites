Our Story

Welcome to Burger Haven

Founded in 2010, Burger Haven started as a small food truck with big dreams. Our mission was simple: create the most delicious, high-quality fast food using fresh, locally-sourced ingredients.

Today, we're proud to have grown into one of the most beloved burger joints in the city, winning multiple awards for our innovative recipes and commitment to sustainability.

Every day, our team of talented chefs handcraft each burger with care, ensuring that every bite is packed with flavor and quality you can taste.
